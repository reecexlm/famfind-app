Why we built FamFind

Your kids' location shouldn't be a product.

The most popular family safety app was found to be selling precise location data on its tens of millions of users, children included, to data brokers who resold it to nearly anyone.

Investigation by The Markup, December 2021

Parents install a family locator for one reason: peace of mind. But most family locators work the same way. Every location ping from every phone goes up to a company's servers, where it's stored, analysed and, too often, monetised. That data has been sold to brokers and has turned up in insurance driving-data deals.

Locations reveal where your children go to school, where you sleep, who you visit and when the house is empty. It's some of the most sensitive data there is, and once it's sold you can't take it back.

So we built FamFind differently. Not with a promise to behave, but with a design where there's nothing for us to collect. Your family's map is built between your family's phones, end-to-end encrypted. We never see it, so it can never be sold, leaked from our servers, or handed over.

How it works

The technology

A family locator with no server in the middle.

Most location apps are a thin front end on a big database. FamFind turns that inside out: each family's map is a shared, encrypted document that lives on the family's own phones and syncs directly between them. It's built on the same local-first, peer-to-peer ideas behind the most private messaging and collaboration tools.

peer-to-peer · iroh · QUIC

Phones talk directly to each other

FamFind connects family members' phones with encrypted QUIC links, punching through home routers and mobile networks so data takes the shortest path. When a direct path isn't possible, traffic is forwarded by a relay that only ever sees encrypted packets.

local-first · Automerge CRDTs

Each family is a shared document

Locations, places, chat and safety state live in conflict-free replicated data types. Every phone keeps its own full copy, works offline, and merges changes from others automatically, with no server deciding who's right.

identity = a key on your phone

No accounts, no passwords to leak

Your identity is a cryptographic key generated on your iPhone. No email, no phone number, nothing to breach. Back it up with a 24-word recovery phrase. When someone leaves or is removed, the family's keys rotate so they can't keep listening.

decentralized invites · Nostr

Invites without a sign-up server

Invite codes are sealed with AES-256-GCM and posted to public, decentralized relays only until they're used. Each code works once, expires in 24 hours, and an admin approves every new member.

Rust core · on-device intelligence

The smarts run on the phone

A shared Rust core handles networking, encryption and sync. Place alerts, trip history, check-ins and crash detection all run on your iPhone, not in a cloud you have to trust.

designed to be verifiable

Privacy you don't have to take on faith

There's no analytics SDK and no push-notification server: every alert is created locally on the phone. Apple's privacy label for FamFind reads "Data Not Collected", because there's no FamFind backend to collect anything.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

If it's free and has no ads, how does FamFind make money?

It doesn't need much. Because there's no central server storing everyone's location, FamFind costs very little to run. We would rather keep it free than turn your family into the product.

Can FamFind see where my family is?

No. Locations travel directly between your family's phones, end-to-end encrypted. When phones can't reach each other directly, encrypted traffic passes through a relay that can't read it. We run no database of locations.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up, email or phone number. Your identity is a key that stays on your phone, and you can back it up with a 24-word recovery phrase.

How does someone join my family?

Share a one-time invite code. It expires after 24 hours, works once, and a family admin approves each new member before they can see anyone.

Can my kids see who's watching them?

Yes. Everyone in a family can see who else is in it, people are told when an alert is set for them, and you're notified when someone opens your location history. FamFind is for families, not for secret tracking.

Does FamFind call emergency services?

No. SOS alerts your family with your live location and gives you one tap to call your local emergency number or text your emergency contacts. In an emergency, always call your local emergency number.

Is there an Android version?

FamFind is on iPhone today.
